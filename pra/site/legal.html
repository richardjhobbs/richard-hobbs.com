Use of artificial intelligence

PRA uses artificial intelligence (AI) tools to support its advisors. These tools help with reading, searching, translating and drafting. They do not replace professional judgement. A named PRA advisor reviews and is responsible for every proposal, report and piece of advice we issue, and no decision about a client or an individual is made by automated means alone.

Information processed with AI. Documents and information you provide for an engagement may be processed by AI tools for the purpose of delivering that engagement. This can include Personal Data contained in those documents.

Where the information goes. Your documents are stored on systems controlled by PRA. When an AI tool reads a document, its content is sent to our AI service provider for processing. This is done under commercial terms that prohibit the provider from using the content to train its models. We do not sell your information or share it with any other party for their own purposes.

Confidential engagements. Information from one client engagement is not disclosed to another client. PRA may draw on its own past work to scope and price new work, and does so without revealing client names or findings.

This website. The "Ask PRA" panel and the connection for AI assistants on this website draw only on information PRA has published. They have no access to client documents or records. If you send an enquiry through the website form, or ask your AI assistant to send one for you, we keep your name, email address, organisation and message so that an advisor can reply. Enquiries are answered by a person.

Your information. To ask what information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, please contact us.
