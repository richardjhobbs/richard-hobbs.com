    How PRA runs

    Advisors lead. Agents do the legwork.

    A due diligence used to spend most of its hours on reading and retyping. PRA has handed that to software, so the people you hired spend their time on your sites and your decisions.

    An engagement, start to finish
    1. You

      Send a brief or terms of reference.

    2. Agent

      Reads the brief in full and finds the closest past PRA engagements.

    3. Advisor

      A director sets the scope, the team and the fee.

    4. Agent

      Reads the data room: every file, scans and other languages included.

    5. Advisor

      Visits the sites with a local specialist. Interviews management.

    6. Agent

      Logs photos, voice notes and observations to the engagement the same day.

    7. Agent

      Assembles the draft. Every finding links to its evidence.

    8. Advisor

      Reviews, makes the judgement calls and signs.

    9. You

      Receive a report you can trace line by line.

    Engagement memory

    Your brief meets a decade of PRA's own work.

    Ten years of proposals, budgets and projects are held as one structured record per engagement. When a new request arrives, an analyst agent finds the jobs most like it before anyone writes a word.

    • Scope drawn from what similar engagements needed, not from a template.
    • Fees set against the real effort of comparable work.
    • The advisors who have done it before, put forward first.
    • Every figure in the proposal traceable to its source file.

        Alliances and partners

        Pacific Risk Advisors was founded in 2005 by James Pearson, who has advised on environmental and social risk in Asia since 1992 and in Europe from 1988. The firm has grown into one of Asia's leading specialists in ESG and sustainability risk for the global investment and business community.

        The approach is pro-investment. Identify, manage and mitigate ESG risk so that the pressure comes off the fund manager and the portfolio company, and the value of the investment rises with it.

        All staff and contractors follow the PRA Principle Codes of Business, which build ESG factors into every service the firm provides.
