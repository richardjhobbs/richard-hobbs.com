Introduction

Risk is changing faster than ever.

Standards are tightening, investors expect more, and climate, supply-chain and human rights issues now reach every boardroom.

Pacific Risk Advisors helps you see what's coming and act early. We combine local knowledge in every market, deep regional understanding, and global foresight on where regulation and investor expectations are heading. The result is practical advice that protects people, the environment and your returns.

Our vision

To be the trusted ESG advisor that brings global foresight to ESG risk, helping investors and businesses worldwide anticipate change and turn risk into resilience, compliance and lasting value.

What we do

Global services that protect and create value.

ESG risk advisory and strategy

ESG strategy, materiality, policies, frameworks and board advisory.

ESG compliance and audits

Environmental, health and safety compliance audits, ISO 14001 and 45001 management systems, fire and life safety, and regulatory registers.

E&S management systems

ESMS for funds, banks and companies, aligned with IFC, development finance institution (DFI) and investor standards.

Transaction services

ESG due diligence for international M&A, red-flag reviews, action plans and post-deal support.

Climate and decarbonisation

Climate risk and scenario analysis, GHG footprinting, transition planning and climate value-at-risk, with our partner Intensel.

Risk management and resilience

Enterprise and operational risk, business continuity, and crisis and emergency preparedness.

Waste and resource advisory

Waste and hazardous waste strategy, circular economy, resource efficiency, and contractor and facility audits.

Social, human rights and safeguarding

Labour and supply-chain due diligence, modern slavery, child protection and PSEAH, and community and land issues.

ESG reporting and analysis platforms

Through our sister company, Clean Future Technologies Ltd (CFT), we provide ESG reporting and analysis platforms. They help you collect, analyse and report ESG data across your operations, portfolio or supply chain, from KPIs and dashboards to investor and regulatory disclosures. Our advisors set up the right metrics, and CFT's platforms make tracking and reporting simple and verifiable.

Technical services

Site assessments and audits, site investigation and remediation, sampling and analysis, HAZOP and HAZID studies, and ESAP verification.

Capacity building and executive coaching

We build ESG capability at every level of your organisation. For boards and senior executives, one-to-one coaching and briefings on ESG risk, investor expectations and incoming regulation. For management and operating teams, workshops and training programmes tailored to your sector and the standards you work to. For institutions, training of trainers, so the knowledge stays with your people.

How we work today

The same advisors, with more time for your sites and your decisions.

PRA is introducing AI agents to support its advisors. They take on the reading, searching and record-keeping. The scope, the site work and the judgement remain with the people you engage.

An engagement, start to finish
PRA advisorsPRA agents
  1. You

    Send a brief or terms of reference.

  2. Agent

    Reads the brief in full and finds comparable past PRA work.

  3. Advisor

    Sets the scope, the team and the fee.

  4. Agent

    Reads the documents: every file, scans and other languages included.

  5. Advisor

    Visits the sites with a local expert. Interviews management.

  6. Agent

    Logs photos, notes and observations the same day.

  7. Advisor

    Reviews the draft, makes the judgement calls and signs.

  8. You

    Receive advice you can trace to its evidence.

What does not change

  • A named advisor signs every deliverable. Agents draft. Advisors review, decide and put their name to the work.
  • Nothing leaves PRA without approval. Proposals, reports and messages wait until a person releases them.
  • Every figure cites its source. Gaps are flagged, never filled by guesswork.
  • Your files stay under PRA's control. When a model reads a document it does so under commercial terms that bar training on it.
  • Public and confidential are kept apart. The assistant on this site sees published information only.
